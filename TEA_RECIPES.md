# Tea Recipes

## Soothing Chamomile Tea

### Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon dried chamomile flowers (or 1 chamomile tea bag)
- 1 cup freshly boiled water
- 1 teaspoon honey (optional)
- A squeeze of fresh lemon (optional)

### Instructions

1. Bring fresh water to a boil and let it cool for about 30 seconds.
2. Place the chamomile flowers in a tea infuser or use a tea bag.
3. Pour the hot water over the chamomile and cover the cup.
4. Steep for 5 minutes for a mild flavor, or up to 10 minutes for a stronger brew.
5. Remove the infuser or tea bag.
6. Stir in honey and lemon if desired.
7. Let it cool to a comfortable drinking temperature.

### A Gentle Reminder

Sip slowly. Let each warm sip settle in before reaching for the next. Tea is best enjoyed without rush — give yourself the quiet moment you deserve.
